TODAY'S ROW (anatomy): 52 px tall. Line 1: title (13/600, one line, tail-truncated). Line 2: a source glyph (a 12 px square with a letter: T = this app, also Telegram / cron / terminal glyphs) + age in mono ("now", "5 min", "1 h", "yesterday", "2 d"). Right side, vertically centred: `RUN 00:36` (label + mono elapsed time, ticking) while a run goes, or a magenta `APPROVAL` chip while an approval waits, or a chevron on the selected row. Top-right corner: a `…` menu button (only under the pointer and on the selected row) and a pin glyph on pinned rows. Bottom-right: up to two tag chips (mono, sunken) then `+N`. A lock glyph after the age on read-only rows (cron).

PROBLEMS seen today:
1. The top-right corner is crowded: `…` and the pin glyph sit above the RUN timer, and on the selected row the pin overlaps the timer's digits (`00:3̶6`).
2. Pinned rows sit under a PINNED head and still repeat a pin glyph on every row.
3. State is weak on the grey rows: `RUN` turns plain white there, so a running conversation doesn't stand out unless it is selected.
4. There is no "finished while you weren't looking" state yet (new, see below).
5. The row is busy for what it says: three corners hold separate marks (…/pin, status, tags).
