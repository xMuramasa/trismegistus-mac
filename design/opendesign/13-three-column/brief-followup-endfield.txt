WHAT MAKES #a2 TOONISH, AND THE RULE FOR #a3 (from comparing with the game screens):
1. Black ink everywhere (card foot bars, ink slab heads, 1 px ink edges on stickers, tags, filter, "+"). → No black outlines or black bars on light grounds. Edges are light-grey hairlines (rule #D9D9D9, border-inactive #C8C8C0, 1 px or 0.5 px) or none; separation by close grey steps. Ink #191919 only for text and for the one dark surface (the section panel), and even that panel reads like the game's dark translucent panels, not a black slab.
2. Saturated colour blocks. → Yellow is a thin line, not a fill: the selected row/card is the surface's own grey or white with a 1-2 px yellow frame (and at most a faint yellow glow), as the game's selected mail item and FPS row do. Solid yellow only on small things: the current tab marker, a small NEW-style tag, a progress line, the one primary button. Status (approval, run, failed, done) becomes small, quiet tags: a 16-18 px tall label with a thin coloured left bar or a small coloured glyph on a light grey chip, word in small caps; magenta only as a thin bar/diamond, never a big fill.
3. Heavy, uppercase type. → Titles in Inter 500-600, sentence/normal case, 13-14 px; list meta 11-12 px regular in secondary grey; uppercase only for tiny labels with tracking, in the game's "// MAIL"-style (e.g. `// CHATS`, `// PINNED`, `// APP · 10`). No 800/900 weights except, at most, one large numeral.
4. Offset drop shadows. → None. Depth from layered translucent/frosted greys (the panel and the list column can be slightly translucent over the paper ground with its contour lines faintly showing through) and from the selected item's thin glow.
5. Few mid-tones. → Work in the game's greys: paper #F2F2F2, raised #FFFFFF, sunken #E5E5E5, rule #D9D9D9, border-inactive #C8C8C0, muted #999999, secondary #666666, dark-panel #35373C; soft vertical gradients between neighbouring greys are fine (subtle, like the game's panels).
6. Missing fine detail. → Add the game's precise small details, sparingly and functional where possible: `//` section labels, small index numbers (01, 02…) or ids in muted grey, a short diagonal hatch at the end of a header or the selected row, tiny tick marks or bracket corners on the selected card or the panel, a thin title rule with a small yellow segment, small metadata (run id, `seq`, ms, timestamps) in the detail header. The Endfield contour lines on the paper ground stay.
7. Chunky rounded shapes. → Smaller avatars (24-28 px), small tags instead of stickers, buttons as thin-edged rectangles; round only for controls (toggles, the model dropdown, radio/check marks) as the game does.
8. Chunky spacing. → Rows 44-48 px with small text; airiness from space around panels, not inside them. The list should fit noticeably more conversations than #a2.
