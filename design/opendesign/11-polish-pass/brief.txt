Prototype: a polish pass on Trismegistus, a native macOS (SwiftUI) control panel for the AI agent "Hermes". Build ONE self-contained index.html that draws the app's main window at exactly 1560x1122 CSS px (macOS points) with all six sections. No device frame, no page around it, no presentation chrome: the window fills the viewport edge to edge.

Navigation: the section icon bar at the sidebar's bottom switches sections, and so does the URL hash: #chat, #github (an open issue), #github-check (the Needs-check inbox with an item selected), #news, #agents, #jobs, #commands. Default #chat. Every hash must render its screen on load (I screenshot each one headless).

ATTACHED: 7 screenshots of the app as it ships today, 1560x1122 at 1x: chat.png, github-issue.png, github-check.png, agents.png, news.png, jobs.png, commands.png. Reuse their real content (conversation titles, messages, issue text, rows, counts, ids) so today vs proposal compares like-for-like. Times are on a 24-hour clock (the screenshots' "01:42" is a bug, read it as 13:42).

GOAL: keep the app's identity and layout, make every screen clearer and more polished. It will be rebuilt in SwiftUI, so everything must be buildable natively: flat fills, 1 px and 0.5 px hairlines, the tokens below; no blur, no glass, no web-only effects, no colours outside the list.

FIXED (the owner decided these; keep them):
- Light mode only. Fonts: Inter (UI) and JetBrains Mono (data: ids, times, counts, commands), from Google Fonts. This app does NOT use the design system's Space Grotesk / Outfit / Archivo.
- Top bar 52 px, edge to edge over the sidebar too: steel gradient left to right #666666 -> #35373C -> #191919, text #E9E9E9. Left: macOS traffic lights, sidebar toggle, the TRISMEGISTUS mark. Right edge: green dot + LINKED + host (mono), terminal toggle, settings.
- Sidebar 304 px: our own flat column on #666666, rows without fills or separators, titles #FFFFFF, meta #E9E9E9; the selected row is a white card with a 1 px yellow #FFFA00 inner border (no left bar) and ink text. Header label like `APP // 10` (label role, uppercase), a filter field, then the list. "macbook · paired" line, then the section icon bar at the bottom: six bare icons (chat bubble, checklist, newspaper, cpu, calendar, terminal), the selected one white with a 2 px yellow bar under it.
- Detail header: steel band with the title (15/600) and a mono subtitle; under it Endfield's title rule (4 px: magenta #FF1AAC 48 px, yellow #FFFA00 120 px, green #00FFA2 the rest), no hairline.
- Detail ground: paper #F2F2F2 with a sparse field of + marks and faint contour lines, as in the screenshots.
- Chat: 700 px column; your messages in white boxes; Hermes' answers as plain text inset 10 px (no bubble, no bar, no box, no markers); labels `YOU // 13:42`, `HERMES // 13:49`.
- Lists of tool calls / commands are Arknights item cards: orange #E86600 header with white text, white body, a cyan #1FE5E0 / magenta #DA16DC / yellow accent column flush on the right edge, rounded only at the top-right (8 px), outlined by a faint shadow (ink 16%, radius 5, y 1) instead of a stroke, folded by default, 24 px apart.
- Buttons: square chips, 1 px edge, word then a trailing icon; yellow fill = the single primary action on a screen; obvious actions icon-only (tooltip). Chevrons: a down chevron on a folded item, an up chevron on an open one.

TOKENS (use only these):
- Colours: paper #F2F2F2, raised #FFFFFF, sunken #E5E5E5, rule #D9D9D9, ink #191919, dark-panel #35373C, secondary #666666, muted #999999, yellow #FFFA00, blue #0040E8 (links), green #00FFA2 (online), magenta #FF1AAC (marker), red #E5332A (errors), border-inactive #C8C8C0, terminal-green #00875A (exit 0), terminal-red #C4271F (failed), ark-orange #E86600, on-steel #E9E9E9, accent-cyan #1FE5E0, accent-magenta #DA16DC.
